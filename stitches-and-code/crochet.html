I first picked up crocheting in high school. Unable to read a pattern or do more than a few basic stitches, I dropped the hobby shortly after my sophomore year in college. In 2022 I unfortunately experienced a second trimester pregnancy loss that shook my world. Around the date my baby should have been born, I decided to try my hand at crocheting again. This time, all the pieces clicked in my brain and I was able to quickly understand and master patterns. Creating objects through crocheting brought comfort and healing as I continued to struggle through grief. In 2023 I welcomed my beautiful son and rainbow baby into this world. crocheting continues to be a channel to help release the stress and fear that comes with motherhood. As I always say, I may not be able to control my life, but I can control what I create.
